Background

Field capture (phone)

GPSoff Compassoff Nearby—
Field mode switches on live GPS and the phone compass. Every photo, video or audio note you take is pinned at your GPS position, pointing the way the phone faced, and linked to any sketch point within the distance below. Media is kept on this device until you export a field pack.

Site walk file

Save this .html file in the same folder as your original photos and videos. Pin positions, headings, comments, marker styling, and your sketch shapes and legend are saved inside the file — but the photos/videos themselves are referenced by filename, not copied in, so the file stays small no matter how many you add. Keep filenames unchanged so they still match up when reopened.
Open saved walk also brings in a newer copy made on another device (for example new sketches drawn on the laptop): you choose whether to update just the sketch, merge everything, or replace what's here.
For phone captures: one ZIP holding the saved site-walk .html, the photo list CSV, and every photo, video and audio file stored on this device, already named to match. Unzip it into one folder on your PC and open the .html.
On iPhone, saving opens the share sheet: choose Save to Files, OneDrive / Google Drive or Mail to get the file to your laptop. To bring new sketches from the laptop, save the walk there, put the .html in Files / OneDrive / Google Drive, then use Open saved walk here and choose Update the sketch, keep my photos.
In the app, saved files go to the phone's Download/SiteWalk folder, and a Share button lets you send them straight to your laptop (Google Drive, email, Nearby Share), or copy them over USB. To bring new sketches from the laptop, save the walk there, send the .html to the phone and tap it (Open with Site Walk), or use Open saved walk here — then choose Update the sketch, keep my photos.

Photo list export

Downloads a spreadsheet with every photo's number, coordinates, and comment — matching the numbers shown on the map pins.

Add a photo

Click to upload or drag photos, videos or audio here

GPS + compass heading read automatically from the file, if present. iPhone HEIC photos, MP4/MOV video clips and audio recordings are supported.

If a photo or video has no location data, you'll be asked to place it manually. GPS is read automatically from MP4/MOV clips when the camera app embedded it (iPhone .mov files reliably include it; support varies on Android) — screen recordings, edited exports, and location-services-off clips usually won't have it, so expect to place some by hand. Full-size viewing loads the original file by name, so avoid duplicate filenames. Note: HEIC photos display fully in Safari, but may not preview in Chrome/Firefox unless converted to JPEG first — the thumbnail will still show fine everywhere.

Editing pins

  • drag Move a pin — click and drag it to correct its location.
  • ctrl + drag Reorient a pin — hold Ctrl, then drag to swing the arrow to the direction the photo was actually facing.
  • click Open a photo — click its pin, thumbnail, or log entry.
  • touch On a phone, open the photo and use the Direction slider or Use phone compass to reorient it.

Shapes

Tick boxes, Ctrl/⌘-click, or Shift-click for a range (also works on the map) to select several. Then group or restyle them together. Click a group heading to select all its shapes.

    Legend

    Share a locked copy

    Creates a single view-only page to share: your shapes, groups, legend and basemap exactly as shown here. It has no drawing tools and no photo upload, and shapes can't be moved, restyled or deleted. Hidden groups are left out.

    Save and load sketch

    Sketch shapes and the legend are also stored inside the file made by “Save site walk”. Import / update sketch takes the shapes from a newer saved walk (.html) or a GeoJSON file: shapes edited elsewhere are updated, new ones are added, and your photos and the shapes that only exist here are kept.
